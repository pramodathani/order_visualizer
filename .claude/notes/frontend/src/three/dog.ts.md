# frontend/src/three/dog.ts

Dogs roam the meadow ring between 1.25 and 2 times the arena's radius (plus 25 units), so they stay out of the arena and in front of the tree line. They trot at about 2.6 to 3.6 units a second and sprint at 7 to 9 about a third of the time, then sniff about for 0.8 to 4.3 seconds, moving their heads.

Legs swing in diagonal pairs (front left with back right) as dogs trot, the body bobs with each stride, and the tail wags faster while running. Coats are rust, gold, black or cream, and sizes vary by about 40%.
