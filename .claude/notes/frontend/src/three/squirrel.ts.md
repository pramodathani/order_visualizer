# frontend/src/three/squirrel.ts

Squirrels mostly dart to the foot of a nearby tree (the nearest of a few picked at random), otherwise make a short dash across open ground. They bound in quick hops, front and back legs moving in pairs as squirrels do, with the bushy tail made of five tufts curling over the back. On stopping they freeze, and half the time sit up on their haunches.

They are about 0.7 units long, roughly a dog's head, so they are seen best when zoomed in on the tree line.
